## Images to AVOID ❌

- Direct animal/lion photographs (except abstract)
- Generic business people in suits
- Unrelated wildlife (elephants, giraffes, etc.)
- Low-resolution images (<1280x720)
- Images with visible watermarks
- Overly corporate stock photos
- Clipart or cartoon graphics

## Images to PREFER ✅

- Satellite/aerial views of landscapes
- Digital interfaces and dashboards
- Maps and GIS visualizations
- Technology in natural settings
- Data flow diagrams
- Professional research settings
- Abstract technical patterns
- Clean, modern designs

## Pro Tips 💡

1. **Consistency**: Use similar image styles across all slides (all photos OR all illustrations, not mixed)

2. **Aerial Views**: For landscape images, prefer satellite/aerial view over ground-level photos

3. **Technology Context**: Show people USING technology, not just technology alone

4. **Lighting**: Prefer well-lit, bright images over dark moody images

5. **Simplicity**: Clean, uncluttered images work better than busy scenes

6. **Text Space**: Choose images with empty space for text overlays if needed

7. **Aspect Ratio**: Use landscape (16:9) images, not portrait orientation
